# Code of Conduct

The nano project adopts the **Contributor Covenant, version 2.1**, in full and
without modification.

The canonical text is published at
<https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/>.

A signed copy of the Contributor Covenant 2.1 is included in this repository
under `docs/CODE_OF_CONDUCT_v2.1.md` (added in a follow-up commit so the
canonical text is preserved verbatim from the upstream source rather than
re-typed).

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all project spaces — GitHub issues, pull
requests, discussions, the project Discord, and any official social channels —
and to all project events both online and in person. It also applies when an
individual is officially representing the community in public spaces.

## Reporting

Conduct concerns can be reported privately to the project maintainers at
<conduct@nano.rs>. Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers respond
within 5 business days.

For urgent or in-progress incidents during a real-time event, contact any
maintainer directly through the channel where the event is happening.

## Enforcement

The maintainer team applies the **Contributor Covenant Enforcement Guidelines
v2.1** when responding to conduct reports — see the upstream link above for the
escalation tiers (Correction → Warning → Temporary Ban → Permanent Ban).

Decisions are made by maintainer consensus. A reporter may appeal a decision
in writing within 30 days; the appeal is reviewed by maintainers who were not
involved in the original decision.
